Gryffindor

Gryffindor is one of the four houses of Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, founded by Godric Gryffindor. It values courage, bravery, nerve, and chivalry. Members of this house are known for standing up for what is right, even in the face of danger.

The house colors are scarlet and gold, and its emblematic animal is the lion — a symbol of strength and leadership. Many of the wizarding world’s most famous witches and wizards have belonged to Gryffindor, including Harry Potter, Hermione Granger, and Ron Weasley.

Gryffindors are often natural leaders, willing to take risks to protect others and fight for justice.
