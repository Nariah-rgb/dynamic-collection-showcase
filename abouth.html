Hufflepuff

Hufflepuff is one of the four houses of Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, founded by Helga Hufflepuff. It values loyalty, patience, dedication, and hard work.

The house colors are yellow and black, and its emblematic animal is the badger — a symbol of determination and resilience.

Hufflepuffs are dependable and welcoming, always willing to support others and work together as a team.
